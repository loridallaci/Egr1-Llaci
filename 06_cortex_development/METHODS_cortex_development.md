## Public dataset, cell-type annotations, and multiome-object reconstruction

Single-nucleus multiome data (paired snRNA-seq and snATAC-seq) from the developing human neocortex were obtained from Zhu et al. (*Science Advances* 2023, 9:eadg3754; GEO GSE204684), which profiled 45,549 nuclei across six developmental stages. We analyzed the five stages comprising one male and one female donor (late fetal, infant, child, adolescent, adult); the earliest fetal stage was excluded because both of its donors are female (confirmed by *XIST* expression and Y-chromosome coverage), precluding a within-stage sex comparison. Cell-type annotations and per-nucleus metadata were taken from the authors' processed object on the CZ CELLxGENE Discover portal. This deposited object provides gene expression only; analyses of chromatin accessibility — EGR1 motif activity and RENIN — therefore required the paired ATAC modality, which we recovered by reconstructing multiome objects from the original Cell Ranger ARC outputs (`raw_feature_bc_matrix.h5` and `atac_fragments.tsv.gz`; GRCh38). For each stage we started from the raw feature-barcode matrix and retained exactly the barcodes annotated by the authors, matched per donor. Each object comprised an RNA assay (raw counts) and a Signac ChromatinAssay; ATAC peaks were called with MACS2 (`CallPeaks`) and quantified into a peaks assay (`FeatureMatrix`) over the authors' full barcode set, and motif positions were annotated with `AddMotifs` using the JASPAR2020 CORE (vertebrate) collection. Every nucleus inherited the authors' cell-type label and sex. Because the authors' barcodes are all present in the raw (pre-cell-calling) matrix, this procedure recovered 100% of the authors' annotated nuclei for the five analyzed stages (7,856 late fetal; 6,406 infant; 9,111 child; 8,300 adolescent; 7,396 adult; 39,069 nuclei total).

## Dimensionality reduction and cross-sex integration

All UMAP embeddings were computed per stage with male and female nuclei integrated, using Seurat v5.5.1 and Signac v1.16.0 (R 4.4.3). For the RNA modality, counts were log-normalized, 2,000 variable features were selected, data were scaled and reduced by PCA, and male and female layers were integrated by reciprocal PCA (`IntegrateLayers`, method = `RPCAIntegration`); a UMAP was computed on the first 30 integrated dimensions. For the ATAC modality, the peaks assay was processed by term frequency–inverse document frequency normalization (`RunTFIDF`), top-feature selection, and singular value decomposition (latent semantic indexing); male and female datasets were then integrated with reciprocal LSI anchors (`FindIntegrationAnchors`/`IntegrateEmbeddings`, reduction = `rlsi`), and a UMAP was computed on LSI dimensions 2–30 (the first component, which correlates with sequencing depth, was excluded). Nuclei were coloured on these embeddings by sex and by the authors' cell-type labels. Nucleus counts per stage and sex are reported in Supplementary Fig. 2a.

## EGR1 motif activity (chromVAR)

Per-nucleus EGR1 motif accessibility was quantified with chromVAR v1.24.0 (R 4.3.3) for the single EGR1 motif (JASPAR2020 matrix MA0162.4). Peak-by-nucleus count matrices, peak coordinates, and the EGR1 motif match vector were extracted from each restricted multiome object; a `SummarizedExperiment` was constructed, GC bias was computed against BSgenome.Hsapiens.UCSC.hg38 (`addGCBias`), background peak sets were generated (`getBackgroundPeaks`), and bias-corrected deviations were computed for the EGR1 annotation (`computeDeviations`). Because background peaks are motif-independent, single-motif deviations are identical to those from a full motif run. The resulting deviation z-scores were plotted on the integrated RNA (RPCA) UMAP, split by sex, on a shared diverging colour scale centred at zero (limits set to the 99th percentile of |z| to avoid outlier saturation).
